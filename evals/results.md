# Evals: naive analysis vs wise_mcp

Each question answered twice from the same OECD data: by a naive first pass with pandas (filter to the measure code, compute the obvious thing), and by wise_mcp's analysis functions, which the MCP tools call. Where How's Life? 2024 publishes the figure, it is shown too. Generated by `uv run python -m evals.run` from data fetched 2026-09-27.

## Every question

### 1. What was the OECD average life expectancy at birth in 2022?

- **Naive:** 79.31 (mean of 141 rows, 47 areas)
- **wise_mcp:** 80.55 (OECD, 2022)
- **How's Life? 2024:** 80.7 years ("A newborn in 2022 could expect to live 80.7 years")
- **Why they differ:** The naive mean includes partner countries and the women/men rows. How's Life? averages the 38 members' totals only.

### 2. What was the OECD average employment rate in 2023?

- **Naive:** 71.28 (mean of 403 rows, 46 areas)
- **wise_mcp:** 78.39 (OECD, 2023)
- **How's Life? 2024:** 78% ("In 2023, 78% of those aged 25-64 were employed")
- **Why they differ:** Breakdown rows (by sex, age, education) pull the naive mean around.

### 3. How much income did OECD households have left after housing costs in 2022?

- **Naive:** 78.84 (mean of 39 rows, 39 areas)
- **wise_mcp:** 79.05 (OECD 36, 2022)
- **How's Life? 2024:** 79% ("79% of their disposable income left after housing costs")
- **Why they differ:** Same mixing of partners and breakdowns.

### 4. What share of employees worked very long hours in 2022, OECD-wide?

- **Naive:** 6.64 (mean of 243 rows, 41 areas)
- **wise_mcp:** 7.30 (OECD 36, 2022)
- **How's Life? 2024:** about 7.1% ("Around one in 14 employees ... worked long hours")
- **Why they differ:** Same mixing of partners and breakdowns.

### 5. Which three OECD countries have the highest life satisfaction?

- **Naive:** ROU 8.40, SVN 8.10, FIN 8.10 (all 2025)
- **wise_mcp:** MEX 8.45 (2021), ISL 8.13 (2018), COL 7.92 (2022)
- **Why they differ:** The newest year covers only a few countries, and it mixes breakdown rows and partners. Each country's latest year, members only, gives a different top three.

### 6. Which countries have the lowest homicide rates?

- **Naive:** LVA 4.50, LVA 2.40, LTU 2.30 (all 2023)
- **wise_mcp:** IRL 0.10 (2022), JPN 0.20 (2021), ITA 0.40 (2022)
- **Why they differ:** Sorting high-to-low, as for most measures, lists the highest homicide rates as the 'best'. Nothing in the data says lower is better.

### 7. Which country has the smallest gender wage gap?

- **Naive:** LUX -8.68 (2022), COL 0.59 (2024), BEL 0.91 (2022)
- **wise_mcp:** COL 0.59 (2024), BEL 0.91 (2022), DNK 3.34 (2024)
- **Why they differ:** Lowest-first puts a negative gap (women paid more) first. The goal is no gap, so wise_mcp ranks by distance from 0.

### 8. Has life satisfaction in France improved since 2010?

- **Naive:** 2013 7.05 → 2025 7.10: improved by 0.05
- **wise_mcp:** 2013 7.05 → 2025 7.10: no clear change
- **Why they differ:** Any change counts as 'improved' naively. How's Life? needs at least 0.2 points to call it improving, and compares around 2010 with the latest year.

### 9. Has trust in national government in France changed since 2010?

- **Naive:** 2006 34.31 → 2025 37.33: improved by 3.02
- **wise_mcp:** 2010 44.29 → 2025 37.33: deteriorating
- **Why they differ:** The first year in the data is 2006, not 2010, and each value is repeated for every year of a 3-year period: 2025 is the 2023-25 figure.

### 10. Has trust in others fallen in France since 2013?

- **Naive:** 2013 4.99 → 2025 4.00: worsened by 0.99
- **wise_mcp:** 2013 4.99 → 2025 4.00: deteriorating (series break)
- **Why they differ:** The series has breaks (method changes) in 2013, 2021 and 2022, so part of the fall may not be real. The naive answer can't tell.

### 11. How big is the gender gap in feeling safe at night in France?

- **Naive:** No 'gender gap' measure in the data; the total is 72.02% (2025), so no answer
- **wise_mcp:** 11.01 points, men minus women (2025); rank 8/38
- **Why they differ:** The headline indicator is the gap between men and women; it isn't in the data at all and has to be computed from the breakdown by sex.

### 12. What share of people in France lack social support?

- **Naive:** 100 − 87.56 = 12.44% (2025)
- **wise_mcp:** 12.24 Percentage of population aged 15 years or over (2025)
- **Why they differ:** 'Lack of social support' isn't 100 minus 'social support': some answers ('don't know') fall in neither. It has its own measure (7_1_DEP).

### 13. How much greenhouse gas does France emit per person?

- **Naive:** 5.65 Kilogrammes of CO2-equivalent per person (2023)
- **wise_mcp:** 5.65 Tonnes of CO2-equivalent per person (2023)
- **Why they differ:** The OECD labels the unit kilograms, but the values are tonnes: read at face value, France emits 1,000 times less than it does.

### 14. What was France's carbon footprint per person in 2018?

- **Naive:** 8.34 (mean of 2 rows: ['T_CO2E_PS', 'T_CO2_PS'])
- **wise_mcp:** 9.86 Tonnes of CO2-equivalent per person (2018; CO2-equivalent by default)
- **Why they differ:** The code holds two series (CO2 and CO2-equivalent), so a naive mean averages two different units.

### 15. What does measure 1_2 show for France?

- **Naive:** 'Top average household disposable income quintile': 4.56 Factor of lowest income quintile (2023)
- **wise_mcp:** S80/S20 income share ratio: 4.56 Factor of lowest income quintile (2023). Average income of the top 20% divided by that of the bottom 20%, despite the API label.
- **Why they differ:** The API calls it 'Top average household disposable income quintile'. It is the S80/S20 ratio: the top 20%'s income divided by the bottom 20%'s.

### 16. What is the OECD average trust in others?

- **Naive:** 5.72, 'the OECD average' over 28 areas
- **wise_mcp:** 5.68 (OECD 30, latest years 2018-2025)
- **Why they differ:** How's Life? averages members with data, each at its latest year, and says how many ('OECD 30'). The naive mean is over whoever has the newest year, partner countries (Bulgaria, Croatia, Romania) included.

### 17. Where does France rank on renewable energy supply?

- **Naive:** rank 32 of 44 (the list includes OECD, BRA, ROU, BGR, ARG)
- **wise_mcp:** rank 29 of 38 OECD members (2024)
- **Why they differ:** Future well-being includes a row for 'OECD' itself, which a naive ranking counts as a country, and partner countries sit in the same table.

### 18. What share of employees in France face job strain?

- **Naive:** 25.79% of employees, 'latest'
- **wise_mcp:** 25.79% (2015). The most recent data for this measure are from 2015.
- **Why they differ:** Job strain stopped in 2015. The naive 'latest' value is a decade old with no warning.

### 19. Where does France rank on overall well-being?

- **Naive:** No overall score in the data: it needs the 36 headline indicators, their direction and the report's rescaling, none of which is in the CSV
- **wise_mcp:** rank 17 of 38 on Better Life 36 (current well-being), score 0.62
- **Why they differ:** The report's overall picture rescales the 36 headline indicators (which ones, and which direction is better, are only in the report) and averages by dimension.

### 20. How many of the headline indicators have recent data?

- **Naive:** Can't tell: which measures are headlines is only in the report
- **wise_mcp:** 24 current-well-being headline indicators with data after 2019
- **Why they differ:** Which 36 of the 103 measures are headline indicators is listed only in the report.
